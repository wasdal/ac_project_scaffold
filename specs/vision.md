# Product Vision

An Ambient Intelligence (AmI) system for detecting risk conditions.

Processing data coming from IoT sensors and cameras, the system identifies possible risk conditions according to the purpose and tasks performed inside each ambient of the target Environment.

The system is designed to support multiple environment types (smart buildings, elderly care, industrial, public spaces, etc).

Examples of Environment:

- An aircraft cockpit
- The floor of an industry
- An school and its classrooms and spaces
- A home and its rooms
- The kitchen of a restaurant
- A public transport vehicule
- A guard post
- The operating room of a hospital

When some risk condition is detected, the system can:

- Generate real time alerts for humans.
- Perform some action in the ambient, through its actuator devices.
