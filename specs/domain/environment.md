## Definition

The environment that the System monitors seeking for risk contitions. Each Environment can have several ambients to be monitored.

## Examples

- An aircraft cockpit
- The floor of an industry
- An school and its classrooms and spaces
- A home and its rooms
- The kitchen of a restaurant
- A public transport vehicule
- A guard post
- The operating room of a hospital

## Attributes

- ambients: list of ambients
